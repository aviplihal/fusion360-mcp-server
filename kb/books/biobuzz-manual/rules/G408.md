title: Don’t CONTROL opponent NECTAR.
page: 108
source: BIOBUZZ competition manual TU05
---

Don’t CONTROL opponent NECTAR. A ROBOT may not CONTROL the opponent’s NECTAR.
Violation: VERBAL WARNING. YELLOW CARD per MATCH, if STRATEGIC.
REPEATED instances of a ROBOT CONTROLLING an opponent’s NECTAR are likely to be perceived as STRATEGIC, even if the ROBOT has a sorting mechanism that automatically discards opponent NECTAR by returning it to approximately its original state.
