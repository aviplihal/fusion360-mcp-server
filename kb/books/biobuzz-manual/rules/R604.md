---
id: R604
title: Fuses must be used as directed and must not be modified.
page: 136
source: BIOBUZZ competition manual TU05
---

Fuses must be used as directed and must not be modified. The intent of this rule is that teams utilize fuses as directed by a device manufacturer, and do not attempt to gain a competitive advantage by modifying the fuses, replacing the fuses with ones that have a higher trip point, or replacing them with fuses that are self-resetting.
