Custom Circuits must not provide regulated power above 5V unless they are only powering LEDs.
Any active electrical item that is not an actuator (specified in R501) or power regulation device (specified in R505) is considered a CUSTOM CIRCUIT. CUSTOM CIRCUITS shall not provide regulated output voltages exceeding 5V, except if solely used for powering LEDs. CUSTOM CIRCUITS may pass through unregulated battery voltage.
