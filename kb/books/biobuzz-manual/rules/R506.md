id: R506
title: The use of relays, electromagnets, and electrical solenoid actuators is prohibited.
page: 135
source: BIOBUZZ competition manual TU05
---

The use of relays, electromagnets, and electrical solenoid actuators is prohibited. The application of electromechanical actuation through the use of additional relays, electromagnets, electrical solenoid actuators, or related systems is prohibited. In addition, the use of relays and electromagnets is also prohibited.
