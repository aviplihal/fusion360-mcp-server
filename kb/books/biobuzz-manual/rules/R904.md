ROBOT application wireless communication only. Other than the connection controlled by the ROBOT CONTROLLER app running on the ROBOT and the DRIVER STATION app running on the DRIVER STATION device, no other form of wireless communications shall be used to communicate to, from, or within the OPERATOR CONSOLE during a MATCH.
Examples of prohibited wireless systems include, but are not limited to, active wireless network cards, wireless gamepad communication, and Bluetooth devices.
