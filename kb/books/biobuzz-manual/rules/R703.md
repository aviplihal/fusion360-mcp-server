---
id: R703
title: Smartphone Android devices used as a ROBOT CONTROLLER must connect to the REV Expansion
page: 141
source: BIOBUZZ competition manual TU05
---

Smartphone Android devices used as a ROBOT CONTROLLER must connect to the REV Expansion Hub using USB. If used as a ROBOT CONTROLLER, the smartphone Android device must be connected via its integrated USB port to a REV Expansion Hub with any combination of USB cables (including USB OTG cables) and/or USB hubs (powered or unpowered, with or without OTG integrated into the hub).
