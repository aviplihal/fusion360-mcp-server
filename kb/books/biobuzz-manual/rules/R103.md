---
id: R103
title: ROBOTS may assist in holding the STARTING CONFIGURATION.
page: 122
source: BIOBUZZ competition manual TU05
---

ROBOTS may assist in holding the STARTING CONFIGURATION. In the STARTING CONFIGURATION, ROBOTS must be fully self-supported (i.e., does not exert force on the sides or top of a sizing tool).
ROBOTS may accomplish this using any combination of:
A. mechanical means while powered-off, and/or
B. initializing an OpMode that pre-positions servos and motors to a desired stationary position.
OpMode may control motors and servos to hold their position to maintain the STARTING CONFIGURATION.
ROBOTS holding STARTING CONFIGURATION during inspection or waiting for a MATCH to start may have to do so for several minutes and should limit the possibility of thermal failure (e.g., not having motors stalled against a hard stop).
Teams should be especially cautious when operating a running ROBOT during inspection, please notify the INSPECTOR that the ROBOT is live.
