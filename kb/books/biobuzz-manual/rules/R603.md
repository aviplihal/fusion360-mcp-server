Connect the ROBOT battery though the Main Power Switch. Exactly one main power switch must control all power provided by the ROBOT battery pack to all power regulating devices on the ROBOT (except as specified by R602) such that all the following conditions are met:
A. must be one of the following approved power switches:

B. must be mounted or located where it is accessible to the team, away from high-speed moving parts and pinch hazards, and
C. secondary power switches can be used on the 12V line downstream of the main power switch.
There are no specific location requirements for the main power switch, but it should be located clear of any high-speed moving parts and pinch hazards.
Teams are permitted to mount the main power switch behind removable panels.
The main power switch should be mounted on the ROBOT such that it is protected from ROBOT-to-ROBOT contact to avoid inadvertent actuation or damage.
