title: There is no ROBOT weight limit.
page: 122

There is no ROBOT weight limit. There is no explicit weight limit for FIRST Tech Challenge ROBOTS playing BIOBUZZ.
While there is no official weight limit, teams should still consider the impact of a ROBOT’S weight on various factors including, but not limited to:
− FIELD TILE damage
− battery consumption
− ROBOT transportation
− overall ROBOT performance
