title: ROBOTS can contain only 1 main battery.

ROBOTS can contain only 1 main battery. ROBOTS must contain 1 and only 1 approved 12V NiMH main battery. This battery is only legal source of electrical energy for the ROBOT control system and actuation during the competition and must be unaltered from the battery manufacturer state except as follows:
A. the fuse may be replaced with a COTS equivalent in-line 20A ATM mini blade fuse installed per R610, and
B. installed connectors may be replaced with other popular connectors such as Anderson Powerpole, XT30, or any connector with a comparable power rating.
