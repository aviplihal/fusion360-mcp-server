Certain limits apply to all configurations. The total number of electronics (motors, servos, Android Devices, etc.) used to build all MECHANISMS and base ROBOT, whether they are used on the ROBOT at the same time or not, may not exceed the constraints specified in Section 12 ROBOT Construction Rules (R).
Inspection (I)
