---
id: G102
title: Be careful when interacting with ARENA elements.
page: 99
source: BIOBUZZ competition manual TU05
---

Be careful when interacting with ARENA elements. A team member is prohibited from the following actions with regards to interaction with ARENA elements:
A. climbing on,
B. hanging from,
C. manipulating such that it does not return to its original shape without human intervention, and
D. damaging.
Violation: VERBAL WARNING.
DRIVE TEAM members may brace the FIELD perimeter at any point during the MATCH. Moving the FIELD perimeter out of position is considered a violation of G102.C.
