Use specified wire colors for specific buses. The 12V main power bus and +5V auxiliary bus wires must be color-coded along their entire length. The following colors must be used:
A. positive (e.g., +12VDC / +5V Aux): red, yellow, white, brown, or black-with-stripe, and
B. negative (e.g., common/GND): black or blue.
Note: this rule does not apply to other types of wires including, but not limited to:
motor wiring, signal level wiring (e.g., encoder and sensor wires), servo cables, and extensions, or wires originally attached to legal devices by their manufacturer.
