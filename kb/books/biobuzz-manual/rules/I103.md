---
id: I103
title: A responsible adult must be present for the whole event.
page: 23
source: BIOBUZZ competition manual TU05
---

A responsible adult must be present for the whole event. At least 1, preferably 2, adult(s) responsible for the STUDENT team members must be present at all times during the event. Responsible adult(s) are recommended to be listed on the team roster.
