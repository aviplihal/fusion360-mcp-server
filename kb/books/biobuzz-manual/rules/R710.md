id: R710
title: Lasers are only allowed if they’re part of a sensor, low-energy, and non-visible.
page: 143
source: BIOBUZZ competition manual TU05
---

Lasers are only allowed if they’re part of a sensor, low-energy, and non-visible. Lasers are not allowed unless they meet all of the following criteria:
A. must be part of a sensor,
B. must be rated as IEC/EN 60825-1 ”Class I” or IEC/EN  7 “Exempt,” and
C. must use non-visible spectrum.
