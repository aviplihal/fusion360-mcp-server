id: I301
title: Bring the complete ROBOT and supporting equipment to inspection.
page: 24
source: BIOBUZZ competition manual TU05
---

Bring the complete ROBOT and supporting equipment to inspection. Teams should present the ROBOT and OPERATOR CONSOLE with all COMPONENTS, including decorative parts, that will be used during MATCHES. Teams are not required to use all inspected COMPONENTS in every MATCH, but they should not add any un-inspected COMPONENTS to the ROBOT without re-inspection. Teams should demonstrate to INSPECTORS all ways the ROBOT will be configured for a MATCH.
