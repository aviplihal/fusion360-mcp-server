title: Legal COTS parts and raw materials can be modified.
page: 127
source: BIOBUZZ competition manual TU05

Legal COTS parts and raw materials can be modified. Allowed raw materials and legal COTS parts can be modified (drilled, cut, painted, etc.) as long as no other rules are violated.
Raw materials refers to unfinished building stock including, but not limited to:
A. sheet stock,
B. extruded shapes,
C. metals, plastic, rubber, and wood, and
D. magnets.
