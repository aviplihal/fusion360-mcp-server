---
id: G405
title: Keep SCORING ELEMENTS In bounds.
page: 107
source: BIOBUZZ competition manual TU05
---

Keep SCORING ELEMENTS In bounds. A ROBOT may not deliberately eject a SCORING ELEMENT from the FIELD (either directly or by bouncing it off a FIELD element or another ROBOT).
Violation: MAJOR FOUL per SCORING ELEMENT.
SCORING ELEMENTS that leave the FIELD during scoring attempts or as the result of ROBOT-to-ROBOT interactions are not considered deliberate ejections and are not violations of this rule.
