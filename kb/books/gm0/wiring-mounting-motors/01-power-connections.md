Depending on the vendor, the motors can come with one of the following connectors:

- JST VH connector (REV motors, some AndyMark NeveRest motors)
- 3.5mm Bullet connector (goBILDA motors)
- Anderson PowerPole (some AndyMark NeveRest motors)

JST VH is probably easiest to use, as it is the same connector used by REV Control/Expansion Hubs. It is also locking. PowerPoles are very reliable and sturdy but somewhat bulky. 3.5mm Bullet connectors are very compact, making cables easy to route through openings, but can be disconnected.

Since REV hubs use JST VH connectors, to connect a motor with 3.5mm Bullet or Anderson PowerPole connectors, you need adapters which you can purchase from REV Robotics, goBILDA, and AndyMark.

You can extend or shorten motor power cables by soldering additional length of cable. This is legal as long as you use 18 gauge or thicker cables. Teams can also purchase extender cables to chain multiple pieces of cable together.
