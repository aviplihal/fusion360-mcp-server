**Problem** | Solution
**2 motor drivetrain**; Less power; Less acceleration | **4 motor drivetrain**; Ability to traverse obstacles; Improved power and acceleration
**Pushbot**; Poor agility/maneuverability; Poor top speed | **Mecanum, 6WD, etc.**; More agile, higher top speed; Customizable gear ratio
**Claw**; Controls 1 element at a time; Easy to break | **Intake**; Controls multiple elements at a time; Much more efficient
**Spur gear gearboxes**; Not for high load use cases; Will break under shock load | **Planetary gearboxes**; Better for drivetrains and high load; Resistant to shock loads
**Single/multi-axis arm**; Often needs high gear ratio; More complex than linear extension | **Linear extension**; Generally faster than arms; Much more precise
