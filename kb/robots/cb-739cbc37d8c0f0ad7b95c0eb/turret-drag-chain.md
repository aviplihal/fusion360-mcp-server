Drag-chain turret: wires run through inner and outer Wiring Ring guides around the turret, so it turns without a slip ring. Three Axon Max servos on Compact ServoBlocks drive the Turret Gear through servo gears. Rides on a Kaydon KA060XP0 thin-section bearing; a Melonbotics absolute encoder reads the angle through a gear.
